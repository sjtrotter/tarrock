# Tarrock: The Fool's Reading — a novelization

A novel-length prose rendering of **one player's complete playthrough** of Tarrock —
the True Shuffle run, beginning at the Cliff and ending at the Axis.

This folder is **derived work, not canon**. The single source of truth remains
`docs/design/` and `docs/quests/`; every scene, name, term, and rule here is taken from
those documents, and where the novel and the design docs disagree, the docs win. The
novelization exists to prove the story out at full emotional length — the way a
vertical slice proves the combat out — and to give everyone who works on Tarrock one
continuous read of what the game is trying to feel like.

## The shape of the book

Twenty-two chapters — one per main quest, in this playthrough's own order — plus six
short interludes in other voices (a carnival handbill, letters across the Divide, a
Bastion writ, a ferryman's ledger, wish-well slips, the watchers at the Axis rim). The
narrator is the Querent, for reasons the final chapter makes plain.

## Reading order note

The novel is spoiler-complete: it contains the twist, the confession, the Querent's
identity, and the True Shuffle ending. Read `docs/design/narrative.md` first if you'd
rather meet the design before the delivery.
